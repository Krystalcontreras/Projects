The Central Park Experience

Early-spring cherry blossoms, open-air meadow acoustics, and New York’s premier botanical art walk.

01 • Sheep Meadow Lawn

Cherry Blossom Main Stage

Set against Manhattan’s iconic midtown skyline on the sprawling 15-acre Sheep Meadow lawn, framed by blooming spring sakura canopies and concert-grade spatial sound.

West 66th – 69th St 12 PM – 10 PM
02 • Living Glasshouse

The Botanical Sound Conservatory

An immersive architectural greenhouse pavilion filled with heirloom peonies, orchids, and acoustic foliage where intimate sets and bio-harmonic soundscapes bloom all afternoon.

360° Spatial Audio All Weekend
03 • Sculpture & Culinary

Sunset Meadow Lounge & Art Walk

Stroll past oversized pastel floral sculptures by NYC botanical artists while enjoying curated local tastings, matcha & botanical tea bars, and golden-hour picnic lounges.

16 Floral Installations NYC Culinary Pop-Ups

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Central Park entrances lead to Sheep Meadow for BLOOM? +

Primary festival gates are located at West 67th Street & Central Park West (near Tavern on the Green) and East 69th Street & Fifth Avenue. Easy subway access via the A/B/C/D/1 lines to 59th St–Columbus Circle or B/C to 72nd St.

What are the festival hours for March 20–21, 2027? +

Gates open at 12:00 PM on both Saturday, March 20 and Sunday, March 21, 2027, with headliner performances concluding at 10:00 PM nightly in accordance with NYC Parks curfew guidelines.

Is BLOOM Festival an all-ages event? +

Yes! BLOOM welcomes music and botanical lovers of all ages. Children under 8 enter free with a ticketed adult, and dedicated 21+ botanical cocktail gardens require valid government photo ID.
